## Context

Dakar Sport Shop needed a credible online presence for sports equipment and apparel — not a generic template, but a fast storefront that reflects local buying habits and can evolve with inventory changes without developer dependency for every price update.

## Problem

Retail sites often ship as marketing shells with checkout bolted on late. The risk is the same as internal ops tools: catalog data drifts from what staff can fulfill, and performance on mobile networks matters as much as visual polish.

## Approach

- **Next.js App Router** with server-rendered catalog pages and image-optimized product presentation.
- **Structured product model** — categories, variants, and stock-oriented fields designed for staff updates rather than hard-coded pages.
- **Commerce UX tuned for mobile** — legible typography, minimal friction paths to contact or purchase intent, no heavy animation budget.

## Outcome

A deployable storefront codebase that demonstrates end-to-end ownership of a local commerce surface — from catalog structure to production build and SEO basics.

## Lessons

**Local commerce is operational software with a public face.** The hard part is keeping catalog truth aligned with fulfillment reality, not choosing a payment plugin.

**Performance is trust.** On variable mobile networks, fast listing pages matter more than decorative interaction.
