## Context

Gerpain is bakery distribution software for multi-bakery operations in Senegal — deliveries, cash collections, settlement, commissions, and inventory. It is also the proving ground for **Bocal**: an append-only ledger substrate meant to travel unchanged into other domains (EduPlan fees next).

The stack is migrating from a split Hono + Next.js app toward a unified TanStack Start + oRPC + Drizzle + Better Auth base, with Bocal as a framework-free package.

## Problem

Bakery distribution money work fails when workflow tables become the financial record. Operators need mutable statuses (`pending → validated`); finance needs immutable, balanced history. Mixing the two produces silent drift and unverifiable commissions.

## Approach

- **Dual-layer money model** — workflow tables for operator UX; Bocal ledger for financial truth; atomic post on validate.
- **Multi-tenant by default** — every tenant query scoped by organization and bakery.
- **Repeatable base** — same typed full-stack shape intended for the next ops product, not a one-off bakery app.

## Outcome

Active build: operator workflows plus Bocal as the money substrate. Public deploy pending — URLs marked `*` until production DNS lands.

## Lessons

**Money needs a substrate, not more CRUD.** Agents make screens cheap; balanced append-only movements are the hard part worth owning.

**Gerpain validates Bocal in the field.** If EduPlan can drop in the same ledger unchanged, the thesis is proven — not just documented.
