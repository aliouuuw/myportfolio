## Context

Les Hirondelles needed a credible institutional site and a way for staff to publish without calling a developer for every news post. I shipped a Vite + React front end with a Convex-backed CMS (TipTap editing, media on R2). That delivery relationship is what later opened the EduPlan enrollment pilot — same school network, deeper operational pain.

## Problem

School sites often freeze after launch because content tooling is missing or too heavy. Without a lightweight CMS, news and events rot, and the institution looks abandoned online even when it is active on the ground.

## Approach

- **Lightweight CMS on Convex** — staff can update without a deploy cycle.
- **TipTap for editorial** — familiar document editing for non-technical staff.
- **Ship the public surface first** — trust and visibility before internal ops software.

## Outcome

Site live at [leshirondelles.sn](https://leshirondelles.sn). Admin path marked `*` until the public CMS URL is confirmed. Direct lineage into the EduPlan case study.

## Lessons

**Institutional trust is a content operations problem.** A polished homepage without publish tools dies in a month.

**Relationships compound.** The CMS delivery created the channel for EduPlan — public site work is sometimes the door to operational systems.
