## Context

Asaaman tackles a concrete ops pain: long drone and aerial video is expensive to review by hand. The product direction is semantic search — ask for what you need, land on the relevant clip — with a public site at asaaman.com and an app surface for the search experience.

## Problem

Frame-by-frame review does not scale. Teams collecting aerial footage need retrieval that understands content, not only timestamps and filenames. Without that, footage piles up unused or reviews burn hours.

## Approach

- **Semantic retrieval over scrubbing** — query-led access to long video corpora.
- **Public product surface** — asaaman.com as the narrative and partner entry point.
- **App path for operators** — search experience separate from marketing (public URL pending).

## Outcome

Product site live at [asaaman.com](https://asaaman.com). App URL marked `*` until production deploy is confirmed.

## Lessons

**AI products still need an ops story.** Semantic search only matters if operators can trust results enough to skip the scrub.

**Separate marketing from the instrument.** The site sells the idea; the app has to survive real footage volume.
