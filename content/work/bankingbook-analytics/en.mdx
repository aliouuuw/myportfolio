## Context

BankingBook Analytics builds cloud-native ALM tooling for institutions in the UEMOA zone. I joined as a contract engineer focused on the open-banking API layer and the surrounding product surfaces — bilingual, regulated-adjacent work where contract clarity matters as much as code.

## Problem

Open-banking integrations fail when domain language and cutover sequencing are vague. Marketing sites can look finished while API consumers still hit broken assumptions. The engagement needed API discipline and a clean public cutover, not another brochure.

## Approach

- **API-first delivery** — open-banking contracts and domain cutover for ALM consumers.
- **Bilingual product surfaces** — FR/EN aligned with how UEMOA operators actually work.
- **Corporate migration** — web and mail onto bbafintech.com without treating the site as the product.

## Outcome

Shipped API and cutover work with a live corporate presence at [bbafintech.com](https://bbafintech.com). Proof type: contract engineering narrative plus live site — not a full product case study of the ALM platform itself.

## Lessons

**Fintech credibility is in the contracts.** Clear domain boundaries and cutover sequencing beat a polished marketing shell.

**Bilingual is operational, not decorative.** FR/EN surfaces that match how institutions work reduce support load after launch.
