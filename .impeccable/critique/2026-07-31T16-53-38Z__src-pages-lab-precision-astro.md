## Design Specificity Verdict

**LLM assessment**: Authored instrument metaphor (bezel → screen → chin + lamp), engagement index, and status-only accent discipline are product-specific. Soft-UI lift stacks and gray-on-gray panels still bleed toward interchangeable neo-futurist soft templates; light mode hierarchy is the weakest point.

**Deterministic scan**: 1 finding — `single-font` warning (false positive: Switzer + JetBrains Mono both load). Layout-scope clean. Overlay reported 65 antipatterns in browser (noise vs CLI). All sampled text contrast ≥4.5:1 in both themes. Phone horizontal overflow on filter chips.

## Overall Impression

The instrument is the craft peak. Contrast math is fine; hierarchy is not. Light mode equalizes surfaces until CTAs and chrome share one gray language. The type-state empties the proof promise after the first non-Everest click.

## What's Working

1. Instrument metaphor as one composed object
2. Engagements + builds IA over a flat project dump
3. Accent discipline (status, not decoration)
